Armaf's Odyssey range keeps growing, and most bottles are described as inspired by a better-known fragrance. This guide puts 17 of them in one place with their published notes, the original each one is described as inspired by, and a figure where we have one.

How to read the numbers. Where a figure starts with a ~ it is a reported similarity that was supplied to us. Where a figure says "notes" it is an overlap we calculate between two published note lists, and only when both lists are in our database. Neither is a measure of how alike two fragrances smell, and we have not tested any of them.

Which Odyssey should you start with?

  • Fresh daily wear: Limoni, Aqua, Mega and Homme White Edition are the citrus and aquatic picks.
  • Warm evening wear: Homme, Aoud and Revolution lean spicy, woody or fruity-smoky.
  • Sweet and dessert styles: Mandarin Sky, Candee, Toffee Coffee, Black Forest and Marshmallow cover the gourmand side.
  • Something different: Go Mango and Bahamas are listed as original creations rather than inspired by a named fragrance.

Quick answers

What is the Armaf Odyssey line?

Odyssey is a range of Armaf eau de parfum fragrances, most listed at 100 ml, covering citrus, woody, sweet and tropical styles.

Which Armaf Odyssey is closest to a designer fragrance?

On the figures we hold, Odyssey Mandarin Sky (~99%) and Odyssey Aqua (~98%) carry the highest reported similarity figures. Those are reported figures supplied to us, not our measurements.

Are Armaf Odyssey fragrances for men or women?

It varies by bottle. Several are described as masculine, others as unisex or sweet and playful. Check each listing, and note that gender labels are only a guide.

Are these official dupes of the originals?

They are separate fragrances from Armaf that are described as inspired by the named originals. They are not copies, and fragrances smell different on different people.
