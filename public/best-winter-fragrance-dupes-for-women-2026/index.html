How to wear sweet winter clones

Cold air mutes fragrance, so you can use a little more than in summer. Start with 3 to 4 sprays, and spray on clothes as well as skin if you want it to last. The sweetest ones, like Dulzura and Yara Elixir, are best in the evening or on cozy days. Lionheart Woman and Starwalker Gold are the easier picks for work.
