Fragrantica-style main accords are listed for both. They share white floral, floral, citrus, and powdery. J'adore also lists fruity, sweet, fresh, and aquatic. Floral Pear also lists rose, amber, woody, and musky.

Price and value

Dior J'adore sits in the Not rated tier and Dossier Floral Pear in the Not rated tier. Sizes and sellers vary, so check the live Amazon listing before you buy and compare the price per ml.

Which should you choose?

  • Choose Floral Pear if you want the J'adore direction at a lower price and like rose, amber, and citrus scents.
  • Choose J'adore if you want the original Dior fragrance itself, including its own character and bottle.
  • Either way, sample first if you can. Fragrances smell different on different people, and batches vary.
