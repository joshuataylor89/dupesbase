How to choose

  • Choose Khamrah if you like warm, sweet, spicy scents and want to spend far less.
  • Choose Angels' Share if you want the Kilian original, with its cognac and oak notes.
  • Either way, use fewer sprays at first. Both are described as sweet and rich.

Quick answers

Is Lattafa Khamrah a Kilian Angels' Share dupe?

It is commonly compared to Angels' Share by reviewers and retailers. It is a different fragrance from Lattafa, and its notes list adds dates, nutmeg and tuberose.

Is Khamrah for men or women?

It is listed as unisex.

Which is better for winter?

Both list warm, sweet accords that are usually described as fall and winter scents. We have not tested either.
