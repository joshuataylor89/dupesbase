How to wear winter clones

Cold air mutes fragrance, so these can take a little more than you’d use in summer. Start with 3 to 4 sprays and go up from there, and spray on clothes as well as skin if you want it to last. For the sweetest picks, like After Effect and Khamrah Qahwa, I stick to evenings. For Liam Grey, Rugir and Muharib, the office is fine.

Quick answers

What are the best winter fragrance dupes for men?

My top picks are Maison Asrar Muharib (a Xerjoff Naxos take), French Avenue Amber Empire (inspired by Clive Christian Blonde Amber), Rayhaan Terra (inspired by Amouage Outlands), Armaf Rugir (inspired by Parfums de Marly Layton) and Lattafa Liam Grey (inspired by BDK Gris Charnel).

What is the best Xerjoff Naxos dupe?

For me it is Maison Asrar Muharib. It’s honeyed tobacco, warm spices and aromatic freshness. It is a different fragrance from Naxos, so treat this as my personal pick.

Is Armaf Rugir a good Layton alternative?

In my opinion yes. It has the same kind of fresh spice and creamy, woody vanilla, and I get more compliments in Rugir than in any other fragrance on this list.

Are clones as good as the originals?

They are not the same fragrance, and they usually don’t smell identical. For a fraction of the price they can come close in feel which is why I wear them.

Which winter clone is safest for the office?

Lattafa Liam Grey and Armaf Rugir. Both are easy, comforting and not too loud.

Which one is the best for a date night?

French Avenue After Effect. It’s sweet, boozy and sensual, and it works best in the evening.
