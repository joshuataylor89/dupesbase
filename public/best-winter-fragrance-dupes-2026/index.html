Quick answers

What makes a fragrance a winter fragrance?

Warm notes such as vanilla, amber, cinnamon and spice are usually described as cold-weather scents. This is a general rule of thumb, not a hard line.

Can I wear these in summer?

You can wear any fragrance any time, but heavy sweet and spicy scents can feel strong in heat. Use fewer sprays.
