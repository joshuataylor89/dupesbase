How to shop for a dupe

  • Treat the notes as a starting point. Two fragrances can share many notes and still smell different, because of the ratio of notes and the base materials.
  • Check the concentration and size. EDT, EDP and parfum differ in strength, and sizes vary by listing.
  • Buy from Amazon or an authorized seller. Counterfeits exist for popular originals, so a very low price on a designer bottle is a warning sign.
  • Start small if you can. A sample or small size is a cheap way to find out whether it suits you.

Quick answers

What is a fragrance dupe?

A dupe is a fragrance that people commonly compare to a more expensive one because they share some notes or a similar style. It is a different product from a different brand, not a copy of the original bottle.

What does the notes overlap percentage mean?

It compares the published note lists of two fragrances and shows how much they share. It is a rough guide only and does not measure how similar the two smell.

Are dupes the same as counterfeits?

No. A counterfeit pretends to be the original brand. The alternatives listed here are sold under their own brand names. Our buttons go to Amazon, and you should check the seller and the listing.

Will a dupe smell exactly like the original?

Probably not exactly. Fragrances change from person to person and from batch to batch. Use our notes and the community comparisons as a guide.
